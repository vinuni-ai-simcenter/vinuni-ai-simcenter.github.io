Project Overview

We are a research team at VinUniversity, Vietnam, exploring how artificial intelligence can support simulation-based medical education. AI-SimCenter brings together computer science and health sciences to help students practice clinical conversations and reflect on their performance.

Our work spans virtual patients, emotionally responsive personas, guided debriefing, and assessment support—with educators at the center of the learning process.

Research Directions

01

Voice-Based AI Patients

Conversational virtual patients for practicing history-taking, clinical reasoning, and patient communication.

02

Emotional Patient Personas

Patient personas that bring emotional responses and diverse communication contexts into simulation practice.

Empathy & communication
03

AI-Assisted Debriefing

Identifying key moments in practice sessions to support instructor-led reflection and individualized feedback.

Reflection & feedback
04

Assessment Support

Exploring rubric-based AI feedback as a second opinion for educators evaluating practical clinical skills.

Educator-guided evaluation
